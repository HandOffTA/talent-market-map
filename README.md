# Talent Market Map

> Part of the [HandOffTA](https://github.com/HandOffTA) portfolio: practical AI tools for hiring teams.

## What It Does

Turn a list of target companies into a visual market map organized by company size, location, and hiring activity.

## The Problem It Solves

Target company lists usually live in a spreadsheet nobody looks at twice. Without a visual view of the market, it's hard to prioritize where to source or explain the talent landscape to hiring managers.

## Who It's For

Sourcers and recruiters planning search strategy, TA leaders presenting market insights, and hiring managers who want to understand the competitive landscape.

## How It Works

1. **Start with a company list.** Paste or upload your target companies.
2. **Enrich the data.** Company size, headquarters/locations, and hiring activity signals are added and organized.
3. **Model the data.** SQL structures it for analysis and filtering.
4. **Visualize it.** A Looker or Power BI dashboard maps companies by size, location, and hiring activity so you can spot where to focus.

## Tools Used

- SQL
- Looker, or
- Power BI
- Google Apps Script
